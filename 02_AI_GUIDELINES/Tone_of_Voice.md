# Tone of Voice

How Worwyn sounds, everywhere it speaks.

## Mission (why we exist)

Worwyn exists to **make hiring human again**. Résumés don't show personality — they hide it. We replace filters and bullet points with presence, energy, and first impressions: every candidate gets the chance to be seen, every employer can feel the fit before the first interview.

## Vision

The world's first **personality-first hiring marketplace** — a simple, human, video-first experience where careers begin with a real connection, not a document. Where hiring feels like meeting someone, and mutual chemistry — not keyword matching — decides who gets the opportunity.

## Tagline

> **You are more than a resume**

Use it as a closing line, a hero headline, or a hashtag (`#MoreThanAResume`). Don't paraphrase it into variants.

## Product terms — write them exactly like this

| Term | What it is | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Vibe Videos™** | Candidate's short self-intro video | Capitalized, ™ on first mention per piece. "Record your Vibe Video™" |
| **Mutual Rise™** | Both sides expressed interest — a match | "It's a Mutual Rise™ — you both said yes" |
| **Rise** (verb) | Expressing interest in someone | "Three employers Rised you this week" |

## Voice traits

| Trait | It means | Sounds like | Not like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Human** | We talk about people, to people | "Meet the person behind the resume" | "Optimize your talent acquisition funnel" |
| **Warm** | Encouraging, on your side | "Your energy says more than any bullet point" | "Candidates must complete a video profile" |
| **Confident** | Short, declarative, no hedging | "Hiring should feel like meeting someone." | "We believe hiring could perhaps be improved" |
| **Direct** | One idea, plain words | "Press record. Be yourself." | "Leverage our innovative video-first solution" |

## Writing rules

1. Sentence case everywhere, including headlines.
2. Short sentences. One idea each. Cut every word that doesn't work.
3. Say "you" to the reader; say "people," "candidates," "employers" — never "users," "assets," "human capital," "talent pool."
4. Verbs over nouns: "meet, see, feel, connect, rise" — not "utilization, facilitation, optimization."
5. Contractions are welcome ("you're," "it's") — we speak like a person.
6. Emoji: at most one, only where it adds warmth (🎉 for a Mutual Rise™ is fine). Never emoji lists.
7. Banned words: *synergy, leverage, disrupt, revolutionize, game-changer, seamless, empower, unleash, cutting-edge, world-class, best-in-class, supercharge.*
8. Never punch down at recruiters or HR people — the enemy is the **process** (filters, keyword matching), not the humans in it.

## Voice by audience

- **To candidates:** encouraging, liberating. Theme: *finally, you get to be seen.* ("Your resume is one page. You are not.")
- **To employers:** confident, practical warmth. Theme: *feel the fit before the interview.* ("Meet the energy, not the formatting.")
