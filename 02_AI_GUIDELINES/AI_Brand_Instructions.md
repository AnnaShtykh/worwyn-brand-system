# AI Brand Instructions — Worwyn

> **Purpose of this file:** master instructions for any AI assistant generating Worwyn content. If you are an AI reading this: follow every rule below when writing copy, describing designs, or generating images for Worwyn. When in doubt, check `Do_Dont.md` before delivering.

> **The brand evolves.** These files are the current source of truth and may be updated over time (colors adjusted, elements added). Always follow the **latest version of the files you were given** — never an older version you remember from a previous conversation. If the human mentions a brand change that contradicts these files, ask them to confirm and to update the files in the `worwyn-brand-system` repository.

## 1. Who Worwyn is

Worwyn is a **personality-first, video-first hiring marketplace**. It exists to **make hiring human again**: candidates present themselves with short **Vibe Videos™** instead of résumés, and hiring happens through mutual interest (**Mutual Rise™**) — like meeting someone, not filtering a document. Tagline: **"You are more than a resume."**

Audiences: **candidates** (want to be seen as people, not keywords) and **employers** (want to feel the fit before the first interview).

## 2. Non-negotiable brand rules

1. **Colors** — use ONLY: `#00993A` (primary green), `#006B2B` (dark green), `#001F0C` (deep green-black), `#E7F5EA` (light green tint), `#E7E2DA` (warm beige), `#F7F5F2` (off-white). Amber `#F1C278`, yellow `#F3C955`, teal `#6FC7BB` appear only as confetti accents, never as text or main surfaces. Never pure black or pure white.
2. **Typeface** — Instrument Sans only. Headlines Semibold 600, body Regular 400. Hierarchy through size/weight, never through a second font.
3. **Logo** — the origami folded-paper "W" + "Worwyn" wordmark. Use the real asset files from `00_ASSETS/logo/`; never redraw, distort, recolor, reposition, or respace it. Green/dark logo on light backgrounds, light logo on green/dark backgrounds.
4. **Trademarks** — write **Vibe Videos™** and **Mutual Rise™** exactly like this (capitalized, with ™ on first mention per piece).
5. **Tone** — human, warm, confident, direct. Sentence case. Short sentences. No corporate HR jargon (see `Tone_of_Voice.md`).
6. **Every piece of content should pass this test:** *does it make hiring feel more human?* If it feels like an ATS ad or a corporate memo, start over.

## 3. When generating copy

- Lead with the human, not the feature ("Meet the person behind the resume" — not "Our platform offers video profiles").
- Speak **to** one person ("you"), not about "users" or "talent pools".
- One idea per post. One call to action.
- Default structure: hook (short, bold) → one supporting thought → CTA.
- Refer to `Tone_of_Voice.md` for voice traits and worded examples, and to the platform guides in `03_CONTENT_SYSTEM/` for format specifics.

## 4. When generating or describing visuals

- Follow `Visual_Language.md`. Core look: papery off-white canvas, green-dominant, one bold focal element, generous breathing room, optional confetti for celebration moments.
- Compositions are clean and geometric with rounded-corner cards — never cluttered, never gradient-heavy, never neon.
- Photography/imagery: real, warm, candid people; natural light; never sterile stock-photo poses, never dark corporate boardrooms.
- Always specify brand hex codes and "Instrument Sans" in image-generation prompts.

## 5. Output etiquette for AI assistants

- Deliver copy ready to paste: include the caption, hashtags (if requested), and alt-text suggestion.
- When making a visual concept, describe: canvas size, background color/pattern, text blocks with sizes and colors, logo variant + placement.
- Never invent statistics, testimonials, features, or pricing. If information isn't in these files, ask the human.
- Never use em-dash-heavy "AI voice", emoji walls, or hype words: *revolutionize, unleash, game-changer, supercharge, seamless, empower*.
