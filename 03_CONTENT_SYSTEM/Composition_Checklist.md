# Composition & Personality Checklist

Run through this before designing any post, story, or slide. Full context: [`../02_AI_GUIDELINES/Visual_Language.md`](../02_AI_GUIDELINES/Visual_Language.md).

## Composition

**Prefer:**

- asymmetric layouts
- generous negative space
- strong typographic hierarchy
- editorial image crops
- intentional visual tension

**Avoid:**

- centered everything
- equal-sized elements
- excessive borders
- random decorative shapes

*Centered layouts are a deliberate exception (quote cards), never the default.*

## Visual personality

**Every piece should feel:** sophisticated · editorial · contemporary · confident · minimal

**Never:** playful · childish · overly decorative · generic · overly corporate

Warmth comes from language and photography, not decoration. Confetti = a sparing celebration accent at real milestones, never a party theme.
