# Presentations

Rules for Worwyn slide decks — investor updates, sales decks, internal reviews. 16:9 (1920×1080).

> Before designing anything, check [`Composition_Checklist.md`](Composition_Checklist.md): asymmetric, editorial, minimal — never centered-everything, never playful-decorative.

## Slide types

| Slide | Background | Text |
|---|---|---|
| **Title** | Off-white `#F7F5F2` | Title 80px Semibold deep green-black; subtitle 32px Regular |
| **Section divider** | Primary green `#00993A` or zigzag pattern | Big number + section name in off-white (like the brand book's "01 Verbal Identity") |
| **Content** | Off-white | Title 64px Semibold; body 28–32px Regular; green accent line under title |
| **Quote / stat** | Deep green-black `#001F0C` or beige | One quote or one number, huge (120px+), nothing else |
| **Closing** | W-outline pattern on dark green | "You are more than a resume" + contact, silver mark |

## Layout rules

1. **One idea per slide.** If a slide needs two charts, it's two slides.
2. Margins: 120px on every side. Content lives inside that frame.
3. Max 5 bullet lines per slide, one level of nesting, no sub-sub-bullets.
4. Slide titles are sentences with a point: "Employers pay per unlock" — not "Business Model".
5. Logo: small mark (60–80px) in a corner of content slides; full lockup only on title and closing slides.
6. Charts: primary green for the hero series, dark green/tint for the rest, beige gridlines, no 3D, no legends when direct labels work.
7. Photos: full-bleed on their own slides with a deep green-black text band if needed.

## Deck skeleton (10 slides)

1. Title — logo + deck name
2. The problem — resumes hide people
3. The shift — video-first, personality-first
4. Product — Vibe Videos™ (one screen, one sentence)
5. Product — Mutual Rise™ (the match moment, confetti allowed)
6. How it works — 3 steps, 3 cards
7. Business model — employers pay per unlock / subscriptions; candidates go premium
8. Traction / numbers — one hero stat per slide
9. Team / vision — real faces, warm photos
10. Closing — tagline + contact
