# Instagram

Worwyn on Instagram: where candidates live. Goal — make people feel *seen* and show that hiring can feel human.

> Before designing anything, check [`Composition_Checklist.md`](Composition_Checklist.md): asymmetric, editorial, minimal — never centered-everything, never playful-decorative.

## Formats

| Format | Size |
|---|---|
| Feed post (portrait, default) | 1080×1350 |
| Feed post (square) | 1080×1080 |
| Carousel cover | 1080×1350 |
| Story / Reel cover | 1080×1920 |

## Post types

**1. Statement post** — one bold brand truth on a clean canvas.
*Visual:* off-white bg, headline 88–110px Semibold in deep green-black, one phrase highlighted in primary green, small green mark top-left.
*Caption:* 1–2 short lines expanding the statement + CTA.
*Example headline:* "Your resume is one page. **You are not.**"

**2. Quote / celebration post** — a Mutual Rise™ story or candidate quote.
*Visual:* primary green bg, silver mark, off-white quote text 64–80px; confetti pattern for celebration variants.
*Example:* ""The first interview felt like a second meeting." — Maya, hired via Mutual Rise™"

**3. Tip carousel** — 5–7 slides of practical advice.
*Visual:* cover on light green tint with big headline + "swipe →"; inner slides alternate off-white/beige, one tip per slide (number 600, tip 400).
*Example topic:* "5 Vibe Video™ mistakes (and what to do instead)"

**4. Product moment** — one feature, shown humanly.
*Visual:* beige bg, rounded card with app screenshot or short copy, green CTA pill.
*Example:* "Three employers Rised you this week. See who →"

## Stories & Reels

- Story bg: zigzag pattern or deep green-black; headline at top third; CTA/link sticker at bottom third (safe zones: 250px top, 310px bottom).
- Reel covers: story layout with title only — legible at thumbnail size.
- Reels themselves follow the video-first spirit: candid, subtitled, vertical, ≤30s.

## Captions

Formula: **Hook line** (repeats or sharpens the visual headline) → 1–2 sentences of substance → **CTA** ("Record your Vibe Video™ — link in bio").

- Length: 1–4 short lines. No paragraph walls.
- Hashtags: 3–5 max, after a blank line: `#MoreThanAResume #Worwyn #hiring` + 1–2 topical.
- Alt text: describe the visual in one sentence (accessibility matters — everyone gets seen).

## Cadence starter plan

Mon statement · Wed tip carousel · Fri celebration/quote · Stories 2–3×/week echoing feed content.
