# LinkedIn

Worwyn on LinkedIn: where employers and recruiters live. Goal — make "feel the fit before the interview" a credible, practical idea.

> Before designing anything, check [`Composition_Checklist.md`](Composition_Checklist.md): asymmetric, editorial, minimal — never centered-everything, never playful-decorative.

## Formats

| Format | Size |
|---|---|
| Feed image (square) | 1200×1200 |
| Link/article banner | 1200×627 |
| Document carousel (PDF) | 1080×1350 pages (reuse Instagram carousel layouts) |

## Post types

**1. Perspective post** — a short, confident take on why hiring is broken and how video fixes it.
*Visual:* off-white or beige bg, headline in deep green-black with one green phrase, mark bottom-right.
*Text:* 3–6 short paragraphs, first line is the hook (it's all people see before "…see more").
*Example hook:* "We reject people in 6 seconds. That's how long a resume gets."

**2. Employer proof** — a hiring story with a concrete outcome.
*Visual:* quote card or simple stat card (one number, 600 weight, primary green).
*Example:* "14 days from Vibe Video™ to signed offer. No keyword filters involved."

**3. Product for employers** — one feature, framed as time/quality won.
*Example:* "Watch 20 candidates be themselves before lunch. Feel the fit before the first interview."

**4. Document carousel** — practical guides ("How to evaluate personality, not just skills").

## Writing rules for LinkedIn

- Same voice (human, warm, confident, direct) — slightly more practical, zero corporate jargon even here.
- First line ≤ 12 words, no "I'm excited to announce."
- Short paragraphs (1–2 sentences), blank line between each.
- End with one question or one CTA, never both.
- Hashtags: 2–3, at the end: `#hiring #MoreThanAResume` + one topical.
- Tag real people/companies only with their consent.

## Cadence starter plan

Tue perspective post · Thu employer proof or product post · 1 document carousel every 2 weeks.
