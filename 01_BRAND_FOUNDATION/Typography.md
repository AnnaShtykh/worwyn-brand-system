# Typography

Worwyn uses **one typeface for everything: [Instrument Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Sans)** (free, Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License).

## The two official styles

| Style | Weight | Line height | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Headline** | Semibold **600** | 100% | Titles, headlines, key statements |
| **Body** | Regular **400** | 100%* | Paragraphs, captions, UI text |

*\*The brand book specifies 100% line height for both styles. For long multi-line body paragraphs (4+ lines), 130–140% is acceptable for readability — headlines stay at 100%.*

## Getting the font

- **Web/CSS:** `font-family: "Instrument Sans", Arial, sans-serif;` via [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Sans)
- **Desktop:** download from Google Fonts and install; use **Regular 400** and **SemiBold 600**
- **Fallback** when Instrument Sans is unavailable (e.g., plain-text tools): Arial or Helvetica — never a serif, script, or condensed face

## Suggested scale for social canvases (1080px wide)

| Element | Weight | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Big statement headline | 600 | 88–110px | 2–4 words per line, tight |
| Standard headline | 600 | 64–80px | Post titles |
| Subheadline | 600 | 40–48px | Supporting line under headline |
| Body | 400 | 30–36px | Keep to 3–5 short lines |
| Caption / handle / footer | 400 | 24–26px | `@worwyn`, URLs, footnotes |

## Suggested scale for presentations (1920×1080)

| Element | Weight | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Slide title | 600 | 64–80px |
| Section number/label | 600 | 28px |
| Body text | 400 | 28–32px |
| Footer/page number | 400 | 18px |

## Rules

- Headlines in **sentence case** ("You are more than a resume") — never ALL CAPS for long phrases; short labels (e.g., "NEW") may be caps.
- Do not letter-space (track) headlines; keep the natural tight setting.
- Do not mix other typefaces into layouts; hierarchy comes from **size and weight only** (400 vs 600).
- Text colors follow [`Colors.md`](Colors.md) — Deep Green-Black on light, Off-White on green.
