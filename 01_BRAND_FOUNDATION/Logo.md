# Logo

The Worwyn logo is an **origami folded-paper "W" mark** above the wordmark **Worwyn**. The paper-fold effect is the most distinctive brand element — treat it like a physical object: it has texture, depth, and light.

Asset files: [`../00_ASSETS/logo/`](../00_ASSETS/README.md) · Brand book pages: 05–07 and 13–16.

## The three variants

| Variant | Mark | Wordmark | Use on | Asset |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Signature green origami W | Subtle green gradient | Light backgrounds (off-white, beige, light tint) | `worwyn-logo-primary-light-bg.png`, mark: `worwyn-mark-green.png` |
| **Light** | Solid silver/paper-white origami W | Subtle light gradient | Dark or rich green backgrounds | `worwyn-mark-silver.png` |
| **Dark** | Deep black / dark green origami W | Black or dark green | Light backgrounds, quiet/premium contexts | `worwyn-mark-dark.png` |

Rule of thumb: **light surface → green (or dark) logo; green/dark surface → light logo.** Never green mark on green background.

## Safe area & sizing

- A grid governs the logo's construction; a **safety zone** must separate the logo from other graphic elements and canvas edges on all media. Use the width of one "V" stroke of the W as the minimum clear space on every side.
- Minimum size: keep the wordmark legible — don't go below ~120px lockup width on screen; use the mark alone when space is smaller.
- The mark may be used alone (app icon, avatar, watermark, pattern anchor) — the wordmark alone is not a logo.

## Correct use

- Place on **clean, contrasting backgrounds** that preserve the paper texture, depth, and legibility.
- Solid brand-color surfaces and subtle patterns (with a calm area behind the logo) are ideal.

## Incorrect use — never do these

The logo's measurements and proportions are fixed. **No modifications, under any circumstances.** This restriction also applies to the brand colors.

- ❌ Do not **compress** or **stretch** (never distort proportions)
- ❌ Do not **change the position** of the mark relative to the wordmark
- ❌ Do not **change the distance** between mark and wordmark
- ❌ Do not recolor outside the three official variants
- ❌ Do not place on busy, detailed backgrounds that interfere with the folded-paper details
- ❌ Do not redraw, outline, add effects (shadows, glows), or rotate
